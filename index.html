Tournament Fair Play & Rules

Rules applying to both Official Tournaments and Custom Rooms hosted on Booyah Zone.

Cheating & Emulators

Headshot scripts, aimbots, speed mods, or panel apps result in permanent ban without coin refunds. PC Emulators are blocked unless match mode explicitly states 'Emulator Allowed'.

Room Access Policy

Room ID & Password unlock 10-15 minutes prior to start time. Do not leak credentials to outside players. Unregistered players joining the room will be kicked by room host.

Host Responsibilities

Custom match hosts are required to update Room credentials on time and record end-game scoreboard screenshots to resolve any kill/placement dispute among players.

Support & Discord

Need host permissions or facing registration payment issues? Contact our moderation team on WhatsApp or Discord 24/7.
